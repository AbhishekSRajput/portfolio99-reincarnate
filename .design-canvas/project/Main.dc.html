02 / Inside PixelGhost

Six ways to lookcloser.

How to use every tool
Layers 01 / 06

Compare with precision.

Your design. On the real page.

Add reference images from files, folders, your clipboard or an HTTPS link. Work with multiple layers, adjust position, scale and rotation, then use opacity and blend modes to reveal mismatches.

  • +Files, folders, clipboard or an HTTPS link
  • +Multiple layers, autosaved for each page
  • +Position, scale and rotation
  • +Opacity and blend modes

Hold \ to hide the reference · Arrows nudge 1px

Screenshot · Editor · Steps 02 / 06

Capture the details.

Mark it up. Walk it through.

Screenshot a full page, visible area or element. Add arrows, notes, highlights and blur, or crop in the editor. Combine captures into a Steps walkthrough and save or copy the finished picture.

  • +Full page, visible area, an area or one element
  • +Numbered notes, arrows, boxes, text, highlights, blur, crop
  • +Steps across pages and tabs, finished as one picture
  • +Recent keeps your last twelve, on your device

03 / Free and local

Every feature is free.Your work stays yours.

No account, paid tier or trial. Choose light or dark mode, five accent colors and an interface in eight languages.

Local-first by design.

Workspaces, preferences, captures and score histories stay in your browser profile. No PixelGhost analytics, ads, tracking or uploads to an operator or AI analysis server.

Selected images, fonts, previews and site checks still make web requests. Device frames load the site with its usual browser session. Page scripts may inspect references displayed on that page.

Read the privacy policy
IndexedDB
URL-keyed workspaces, images and named projects, the last twelve screenshots, a Steps walkthrough in progress, and up to twenty SEO deep-scan results per page.
Local storage
Consent, display and tool preferences, recent colors, selector rules and custom devices.
Recordings
Saved to your device and not retained in the extension.
Clipboard
Read access is optional and requested only when you select Paste. Ordinary Ctrl+V works without it.

+ Good to know

Protected browser pages are unsupported; framing restrictions can limit device previews.

Vision effects are approximations, not medical assessments or accessibility certification.

AI answers uses a local, deterministic rubric with same-site probes; scores do not predict rankings or citations.

Stack detection can be incomplete and is not a vulnerability scan.
