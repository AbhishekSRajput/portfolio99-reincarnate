# 👻 PixelGhost Support

Get help with PixelGhost, report a bug, or suggest an improvement through
GitHub Issues:

- [Search existing issues](https://github.com/AbhishekSRajput/pixelghost/issues)
- [Open a new issue](https://github.com/AbhishekSRajput/pixelghost/issues/new)

You can also contact support at <codecube99@gmail.com>.

## 🔎 Before opening an issue

PixelGhost works on ordinary `http://` and `https://` pages. Browser-internal
pages, extension stores, and other restricted pages do not allow extensions
to inject overlays. If the affected page is supported, reload it and try the
action again before reporting the problem.

For keyboard problems, check the widget toggle in Chrome's extension shortcut
settings: `Alt+Shift+G` is the suggested default and may be unassigned if it
conflicts with another shortcut. Color and capture shortcuts require their
tools to be open. See the [shortcut guide](TOOLS.md#keyboard-shortcuts).

Device previews load the site in frames and can be limited by its framing
rules. Report whether **Allow framing**, **High fidelity**, or **Stay on after
reload** was enabled. Device previews use your desktop browser's engine.

## 📝 What to include

- The PixelGhost version and Chrome version.
- Clear steps to reproduce the problem.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- The affected tool: Layers, Color, Ruler, Inspector, Screenshot, its editor
  or Steps, Devices, Element Selector, Assets, Vision, SEO, or Stack.
- Whether the popup or side panel was open, and whether reloading the page
  changes the result. For keyboard issues, include your operating system.
- Screenshots or error messages, if they are safe to share.

## 🔒 Privacy and security

GitHub issues are public. Do not post private overlay images, captures or
recordings, audit exports, sensitive page URLs, personal information, or other
confidential data. Crop or redact anything you choose to share. Send privacy requests,
security reports, or support that requires private details to
<codecube99@gmail.com>.

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for details about how PixelGhost handles
data.
